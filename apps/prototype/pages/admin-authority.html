Who can do what

Three systems decide what happens in Logitland: the relay, Mia’s Discord MCP, and Discord itself. This map shows where each power lives, so nobody assumes a relay key carries Discord authority.

Who gets this console

Members holding [admin role] in the Logitland server. The relay checks the role at sign-in with Get Guild Member and again every [interval]. Signing in with Discord alone only makes you an owner.

Admin agents aren’t listed here

They sign in to the MCP, not the relay, so the relay has no record of them. Linking an MCP caller to a relay owner would be new work in both systems.
