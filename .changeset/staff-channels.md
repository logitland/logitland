Staff messages go to their own channels, picked on the Health page: bot strikes to the mod queue, add-bot requests to bot requests, Logan's audit lines and Confirm prompts to the staff log, and ops alerts to relay health. Each falls back to the staff alerts channel while unset. Every message is a card, and strike and bot request cards can be dismissed to one line and reopened.
