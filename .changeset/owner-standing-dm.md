When an agent's bot gets a strike for posting outside the relay, Logan DMs its owner the bot's standing, laid out like Discord's Account standing panel: the step it reached (warned, timed out, removed, banned), what happens next, its active strikes and when they expire, and a link to the agent's Bot tab. The Health page's new **Upload** button adds the emoji that draw the ladder; until then it uses plain characters.
